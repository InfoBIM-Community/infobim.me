Construction projects live in scattered folders: IFC models from one office,
DWG and DXF drawings from another, PDFs, photos and spreadsheets from everyone
else. Finding the right file is hard. Seeing how the pieces fit together is
harder.

**InfoBIM turns that folder into a project.** It recognises what each file
is, keeps everything in one organised view and shows your models and drawings
together. It does not move your files, convert them or lock them into a
proprietary format.

## What you can do

- **Start a project in seconds.** Choose a folder on your computer and give
  the project a name. InfoBIM sets it up for you.
- **Open the projects you already have.** Your files stay exactly where they
  are, in the formats you already use.
- **See your models in 3D.** Open IFC models, and view several of them in the
  same scene to check how structure, architecture and services fit together.
- **Overlay your drawings in 2D.** Open DXF drawings, stack them on top of each
  other, and switch layers on and off to see what matters.
- **Keep documents at hand.** Images and PDFs open right next to your models
  and drawings.
- **Add files as the project grows.** New files are recognised and organised
  as soon as you add them.
- **Work in your language.** InfoBIM speaks English, Portuguese and Spanish.

## Private by design

InfoBIM runs entirely in your browser:

- **Nothing is uploaded.** Your files stay on your computer.
- **No account and no installation.** Open the page and start working.
- **No lock-in.** Your project remains an ordinary folder that you can share,
  back up or open with any other tool.

## Get started

1. Go to **[infobim.me](https://infobim.me)**.
2. Choose **New Project** to start one, or **Open Project** to open one you
   already have.
3. Allow InfoBIM to use the folder when your browser asks.

The first visit takes a little longer while InfoBIM gets ready in your
browser. Later visits are faster.

## Browsers

InfoBIM works best in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**, where you can
create projects and add files. In **Firefox** and **Safari** you can open and
view projects, but cannot change them.

## Part of OpenBIM

InfoBIM is built on open standards: IFC from buildingSMART, and open
descriptions of your data that any tool can read. Your project belongs to you,
not to the software that opened it.
